Visual Prompt Studio 3.3: The Art Revolution and Redefinition Manifesto


Prompt 

Create a promotional landing page for an exhibition titled “Tradition and Transformation: Breaking Free.” The subject matter should combine Medieval Sacred Art with the modern theme of Freedom and Liberation. Show a figure breaking free from heavy, textured chains inside a medieval-inspired space, surrounded by divine light to symbolize hope and freedom from systemic constraints. Use Pop Art as the main style, combining medieval textures and flat shapes with bold, modern colors. Include contour lines around the figure and directional lines that lead the viewer's eyes toward the light. Use strong contrast between dark shadows and bright light to emphasize the figure breaking free. Include the exhibit title, a short description, and a made-up date and location.


Vocabulary Breakdown 

Contemporary Art: Reflects ideas, experiences, and issues in the modern world.

Pop Art: A style that uses bright colors and pictures from popular culture, advertising, and everyday life.

Directional Lines: Lines that guide my eyes through different parts of an artwork.

Value Contrast: The difference between light and dark areas in an artwork.

Focal Point: The main thing in an artwork that attracts our attention first.

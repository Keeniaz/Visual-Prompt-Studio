Visual Studio 2.1 : Exploring Medium Through Prompt Variation

Prompt A — Painting/Acrylic 

Create an acrylic painting of a basket filled with peonies, emphasizing texture, curved lines, color, and balance. Use overlapping curved lines to create the white basket and fill it with pink, white, and violet peonies. Use acrylic brushes and layered acrylic paint to create the smooth, slightly flat textured surface of the petals. Use layering and controlled brushstrokes to build the colors and details of the flowers, while light and color create a peaceful and grounding mood. 

Prompt B — Engraving 

Create an engraving print of a basket filled with peonies, emphasizing texture, curved lines, color, and balance. Use overlapping curved lines to create the white basket, filling it with pink, white, and violet peonies. Use an engraving tool to incise lines into the printing surface, using cross-hatching and varied line thickness to build texture, shading, and depth in the flowers, while light and color create a peaceful, grounding mood.

Visual Prompt Studio Phase 1.2 : The Immersive Album Cover Challenge

Prompt 

Create an acoustic folk/Americana world album cover featuring a quiet wilderness landscape with a small, old, weathered wooden cabin surrounded by new growing plants, tall pine trees, atmospheric mountains, a calm river, and a pronghorn. Use organic textures like rough tree bark, worn paper, and natural grass to create a handmade and peaceful feeling. Use warm natural light with darker shadows around the trees to create a calm and cozy mood. Create depth by placing the large cabin in the foreground, overlapping trees in the middle, and smaller mountains fading into the background. Show the passage of time with the old cabin and new plants, and create movement with flowing water, drifting leaves, and grass moving in the wind. Use warm earthy colors like green, brown, and gold to create unity. Add an unexpected bright blue flower to create visual interest and variety. Use an asymmetrical balance with the cabin as the largest element while the trees, river, and mountains create balance around it. 

Vocabulary Breakdown

Value: Value is how light or dark something looks.

Space: Space is the area around and between objects that helps show distance or depth.

Unity: Unity is when the different parts of an artwork look like they belong together.

Balance: Balance is when the objects are arranged evenly so the artwork feels balanced and not unfinished.

Rhythm: Rhythm is when lines, shapes, colors, or objects repeat and create a visual pattern or movement.

**Assignment Title** Studio 1.1
**Prompt** Create a science-fiction scene of an overgrown abandoned space station on Neptune. Include extremely tall buildings with sharp geometric lines to make them appear like towering skyscrapers. Add flying automobiles with dramatic directional lines to create the feeling that they are flying and moving quickly through the sky.
The planets in the sky can have various geometric shapes, such as squares and circles. The skyscrapers, automobiles, and planets should have three-dimensional volume and depth. Use positive space to highlight the main subjects and negative space to create a feeling of intensity, isolation, and loneliness.
The buildings should shine against a dark black sunset sky. Include smooth metallic textures on the buildings, and make the roads appear clean, man-made, and smooth with no potholes.
